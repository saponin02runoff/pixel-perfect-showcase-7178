# Strengthen the homepage mission and programme case

## Goal
Make Learning Labs and the reason for prioritising foundational learning unmistakably clear, while preserving Northbridge’s established design, programme safeguards, and evidence-led tone.

## Homepage updates
- Change the main line to **“Stronger foundations. Brighter futures.”** and shorten the supporting sentence as requested.
- Replace the generic location label with a mission-led line centred on the belief that better education builds a better nation.
- Update the Northbridge Consulting endorsement to state that it is a career development consulting firm serving students across 40+ universities in the United States.
- Rebuild **The case for action** as a clearer, more substantial section explaining:
  - the Delhi evidence across mathematics, reading/language, and science;
  - why foundations affect later study, confidence, work, earnings, and contribution to society;
  - why Learning Labs assesses students directly and teaches at their demonstrated level;
  - what Northbridge intends to change without claiming results it has not yet achieved.
- Keep the homepage concise through a strong overview, selected evidence cards, and clear links to Learning Labs and the full evidence page.

## Supporting page updates
- Expand **Learning Labs** with the plain-English rationale, broader learning areas, and the connection between foundations and future opportunity.
- Expand **Evidence & Accountability** with the additional PARAKH, World Bank, and OECD evidence and the exact limitations supplied.
- Add all new source links to the public source library.
- Refine the About mission and vision language around stronger learning, confidence, dignified livelihoods, and contribution to society—without promising those outcomes.

## Contact and shared wording
- Add `contact@northbridgefoundation.org` to the footer’s Connect section as a visible mail link.
- Replace the header announcement and footer description with concise mission-aligned language.
- Preserve the current logo, photographs, visual identity, navigation, forms, existing contact details, and future-programme status labels.

## Validation
- Check all revised claims against their linked sources and keep limitations adjacent to statistics.
- Verify desktop and mobile layout, long links, navigation, email and source links, and clean preview output.
