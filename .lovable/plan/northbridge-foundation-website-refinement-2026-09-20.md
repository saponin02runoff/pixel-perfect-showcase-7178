Preserve the site’s current visual identity and page structure while making the photography, affiliation messaging, program language, and institutional voice feel credible and polished.

### 2. Make the Northbridge Consulting affiliation prominent

- Replace the small homepage footnote with a clear, visible affiliation panel near the top of the homepage.
- Explain that Northbridge Consulting supports students pursuing opportunities in the United States build stronger applications, prepare for interviews, and approach internships and full-time opportunities with clarity.

### 3. Strengthen programs and institutional voice

- Add concrete alumni and professional exposure language to the existing “Show Children More of the World” pillar.
- Add an exposure section describing alumni talks, panels, mentorship sessions, and pathways students can see through people with similar backgrounds.
- Use honest, available actions—nominate a speaker, join as a mentor, or contact the foundation—rather than inventing upcoming events.
- Replace every apologetic or informal passage across Home, About, Our Approach, and Get Involved with confident language centered on mission, delivery standards, partnerships, and measurable student progress.
- Narrow volunteer recruitment to teachers, tutors, mentors, and experienced leaders who can build programs and own outcomes.

### 5. Verify the finished site

- Check all edited copy for the prohibited “new/small/inexperienced” framing.

## Content principles

- Confident, warm, precise, and institutional.
- No unsupported impact numbers, named school partners, board members, donation claims, or scheduled events.
- Preserve the current teal, amber, cream, typography, navigation, and overall layout.
- Follow the clear mission → approach → engagement pattern used by leading education organizations, while avoiding claims the foundation cannot substantiate.

## Technical details

- Keep the existing TanStack routes and shared site components.
- Store optimized local image assets in modern web formats with JPEG fallback only if needed.
- Reuse existing semantic color and button styles; add only focused presentation styles needed for the affiliation and exposure content.
