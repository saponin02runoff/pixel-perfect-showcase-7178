# Northbridge programme website update

## Goal
Expand the existing Northbridge Foundation website around its Delhi programme strategy while preserving the established brand, unrelated content, contact flow, logo, imagery, and working links. Learning Labs will remain the clear present-day focus; all other programme areas will be visibly framed as future plans.

## Pages and navigation
- Update **Home** with a concise Delhi mission, carefully qualified PARAKH evidence, the Assess → Teach → Track progress model, the four-part programme pathway, commitments, and relevant engagement routes.
- Replace **Our Approach** in navigation with **Our Work** at `/our-work`, while keeping `/approach` available as a compatibility page so existing links retain value.
- Add detailed pages for `/learning-labs`, `/future-labs`, `/mentor-network`, and `/build-labs`.
- Add `/evidence-accountability` with evidence context, limitations, monitoring framework, publishing commitments, and the complete clickable source library.
- Add `/safeguarding` with transparent pre-delivery commitments that do not imply unconfirmed systems already exist.
- Update **Get Involved** for schools, future volunteers/mentors, and supporters/funders using “register interest” language.
- Add `/resources` as a restrained updates/resources index and `/privacy` for the footer’s privacy destination.
- Update header and footer links without changing the existing visual identity or contact details.

## Content safeguards
- Give at least 60% of programme-focused content and visual emphasis to Learning Labs.
- Describe Learning Labs as the first/current core programme preparing to launch; do not claim delivery or results not established in the supplied document.
- Mark Future Labs, Mentor Network, and Build Labs as **Future plan** beside titles and throughout summaries.
- Link every published statistic or research-based claim directly to its supporting source and place limitations nearby.
- Present 8–15 students per facilitator only as an initial operating hypothesis.
- State clearly that evidence informs programme design but does not prove Northbridge outcomes.
- Use careful safeguarding wording: Northbridge is establishing required systems before delivery.

## Design and accessibility
- Preserve the existing teal, amber, cream, editorial typography, imagery, spacing, and component language.
- Use calm full-width sections, compact status labels, accessible disclosure panels, responsive tables, and a simple programme sequence.
- Keep semantic heading order, descriptive links, keyboard-operable native controls, visible focus states, meaningful image alternatives, and mobile-safe long URLs.
- Retain the existing contact form and ensure every internal navigation destination exists.

## Technical details
- Add unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata to every new page.
- Add Organisation structured data at the root and appropriate Article/Report structured data to the evidence page.
- Mark public content routes for sitemap inclusion while preserving any current sitemap decisions.
- Validate the complete route set, source links, future-plan labels, prohibited claims, desktop/mobile layout, keyboard navigation, and clean build output.

## Final handoff
Provide a concise inventory of created/updated pages, preserved content, every source link added, and all decisions or confirmations still needed before publication.
